### What is the Gospel? (WORK IN PROGRESS)(NOT TO BE USED)
The word "Gospel" simply means "Good News." But to understand why the news is good, we must understand the story of the world through the lens of Scripture.

#### 1. Creation & Character
God is the sovereign Creator of all things. He is holy, just, and perfectly good. He created us in His image to know Him and reflect His glory.
> "Holy, holy, holy is the Lord of hosts; the whole earth is full of his glory!" — **Isaiah 6:3**

#### 2. The Fall & Rebellion
Though we were made for God, we all chose to rebel against Him. This is what the Bible calls **sin**. Sin isn't just "making a mistake"; it is a rejection of God’s authority. This rebellion separates us from God and leads to spiritual death.
> "For all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God." — **Romans 3:23**

#### 3. Redemption in Christ
Because God is just, He must punish sin. But because He is merciful, He sent His Son, Jesus Christ. Jesus lived the perfect life we couldn't live and died the death we deserved. Three days later, He rose from the grave, proving His victory over sin and death.
> "For our sake he made him to be sin who knew no sin, so that in him we might become the righteousness of God." — **2 Corinthians 5:21**

#### 4. Restoration & Response
The "Good News" is that salvation is not a reward for being "good"; it is a gift of **Grace** received through **Faith**. When we repent of our sins and trust in Christ alone, we are reconciled to God and given eternal life.
